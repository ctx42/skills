# Lessons

- Check every local Glossary entry against the Company Glossary term set: a term the Company Glossary defines (e.g. User Settings) needs no local entry — flag it and link the first use instead (GLO-3/GLO-4).
- Two names for one channel or concept that differ in meaning (water level vs water detection) are a logical/ambiguity finding asking which is meant, not terminology drift asking to "use one name".
