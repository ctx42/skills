# Starforge Terms

## Build Order (BO)

The factory's record of one vehicle to build for one customer mission.

## Customer Mission

A customer's booking: one payload, one launch slot.

## Payload

What the customer sends to orbit; has a mass and a size class.

## Stage

One propulsive section of a vehicle; Spark has two, Ember three.

## Assembly Bay

One of the four build halls, Bay A to Bay D.

## Launch Slot

A 90-minute period booked for one launch.

## Launch Window

The span of days a mission may fly in.

## Part Kit

The set of parts picked for one stage of one build order.

## Scrub

A launch attempt called off before liftoff.

## Mission Manifest

The document listing a mission's payload and slot.

## Hot-Fire

A static firing of an engine or stage at the test site.

## Hot-Fire Campaign

The series of hot-fires that clears a stage to fly.

## Telemetry Channel

One named stream of values a vehicle sends, such as chamber pressure or tank
level.

## Downlink Frame

One timestamped packet of channel values sent to the ground.

## Anomaly

A channel value outside its expected range, flagged for review.

## Booster Turnaround

The work between a booster's landing and its next flight.
