# Starforge Terms

## Hot-Fire

A static firing of an engine or Stage at the test site.

## Hot-Fire Campaign

The series of Hot-Fires that clears a Stage to fly. Campaign hot-fires are
scheduled between 06:00 and 10:00 site time.
