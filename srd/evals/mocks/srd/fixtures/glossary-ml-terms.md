# ML Terms

## Build-Time Forecast

A model's estimate of the days a build order needs from creation to rollout,
per stage.

## Shortage Risk Score

A score from 0 to 1 giving the chance that a part kit is not complete on its
pick date.

## Slot Recommender

A model that ranks the open launch slots in a customer mission's launch window.

## Anomaly Model

A model that flags telemetry channel values outside their expected range as
candidate anomalies.

## Training Window

The span of past data a model is trained on.

## Confidence Band

The range around a forecast that holds the true value with a stated
probability.
